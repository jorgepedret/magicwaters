A weekly nature program for children and families in Sooke, BC.

We meet outside, every Friday, all year round, following the seasons, the weather, and the children, instead of a fixed lesson plan.

See the Friday Immersion Get in Touch

In-Person Program

Every Friday, 10am to 3pm

Children and adults meet in the forest near Sooke, one day a week, all year.

What we actually do that day depends on the season, the weather, the moon, even the time of day.

We use the medicine wheel and the eight directions to shape the flow of a day, instead of a curriculum written months in advance.

Some Fridays are fort-building and animal tracking.

Some are foraging, or fire, or just sitting still long enough to notice what's actually happening around you.

Children run a lot of their own games and make their own rules out there.

That's what we mean by Children's Culture, the culture that shows up on its own when children get real time together in a real place.

Our job is mostly to protect the space for that, not to run the show.

Families come and go with the seasons of their own lives.

New families are always welcome.

Come see a Friday before you decide anything.

Why This Exists

How it started

I started Magic Waters because I wanted my own children to have real time outside, with other children, without an adult running the show for them.

I also started Magic Waters because I wanted a possibility for the adults of the family to talk, to connect, to be with each other, while their children are held in an awesome space.

I've spent years living in intentional communities, doing this kind of work alongside other families.

Magic Waters is what happened when I stopped researching it and just started doing it here, in Sooke, with the people around me.

It's still becoming what it's going to be. That's part of the point.

Jorge, originator of Magic Waters
